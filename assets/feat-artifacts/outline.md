# Draft outline — "idea → proof"

Captured before any of this was formalized; kept here as the proto of the note.

1. The hunch, scribbled on a whiteboard (see `sketch.svg`).
2. Restate it precisely — what are the objects, what is the claim?
3. Try the obvious approach; note exactly where it breaks.
4. Patch the broken step → first real lemma.
5. Reassemble into the argument that ended up in the note.

> These rough drafts are intentionally messier than the published tree —
> that's the point: the artifact panel preserves where it started.

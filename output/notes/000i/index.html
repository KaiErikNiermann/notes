Quiz. Comparing Normal Forms [000i]

  • November 22, 2025
  1. Is a formula that is DNF also in NNF?
  2. How can we check the satisfiability of a formula in DNF?

Solution. 

  • November 22, 2025
  1. Yes. As we are restricted to the same set of connectives in both DNF and NNF, and since DNF also only applies negations to literals, any formula in DNF is also in NNF.
  2. A formula in DNF is satisfiable if at least one of its clauses is satisfiable. Since each clause is a conjunction of literals, a clause is satisfiable if none of its literals contradict each other (i.e., it does not contain both a variable and its negation). Thus, to check the satisfiability of a DNF formula, we can examine each clause individually and see if there exists at least one clause where all literals can be true simultaneously.

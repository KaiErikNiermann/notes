The main part of the proof here is that we instantiate the first induction hypothesis with the weakest precondition for the second statement, i.e.:

\[ ih_1(\texttt {wp}(s_2, Q)) \]

What this gives us is:

\[ \begin {align} \texttt {vc}(s_1, \texttt {wp}(s_2, Q(\sigma ))) &\to \forall \sigma , \sigma '.\ \texttt {wp}(s_1, \texttt {wp}(s_2, Q(\sigma ))) \\ &\to (s_1, \sigma ) \Downarrow \sigma ' \\ &\to \texttt {wp}(s_2, Q(\sigma ')) \end {align} \]

So then if we instantiate the second induction hypothesis with \(Q\) we can get:

\[ \begin {align} \texttt {vc}(s_2, Q) &\to \forall \sigma , \sigma '.\ \texttt {wp}(s_2, Q(\sigma )) \\ &\to (s_2, \sigma ) \Downarrow \sigma ' \\ &\to Q(\sigma ') \end {align} \]
